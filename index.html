Document Information

Exposure (log H) Definition

Assumes every step is exactly 0.15 log H. Real tablets deviate a little; for accuracy use Step Tablet mode with your tablet's certified densities.

Number of log H values must match the number of density values for each curve.

EFS Calculation

Box-speed referenceTransfers a known box speed to your sensitometer: a strip of a film with that box speed, developed to ΔD 0.80, sets the log H offset so its EI reads the box speed. This is not an absolute lux·s calibration. Every EI is only as good as the box speed and the reference's ΔD (keep it within 0.80 ± 0.05). The plot stays in relative log H.

Correct DensitometerRead a calibrated step wedge with your densitometer, then paste the certified densities and your readings. A linear fit then corrects every density reading. This helps when you use different densitometers. The fit is checked ASTM E1079-style: every step should land within ±0.05 D of its certified value.

Paper & Process

Test conditionsDatasheet ISO R values are measured with a ~3000 K source, 0.1–10 s exposures and a stated process. Strips made otherwise compare with them only roughly.

ISO 6846 §5.3 and §7.3: tungsten source near 3000 K, exposure between 0.1 and 10 s, filtration and process stated. Ilford quotes its R values for dish processing.
